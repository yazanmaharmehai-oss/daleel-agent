# Affiliate post pack

Use this after a short promo video is scripted or rendered, to produce everything the person needs to publish it by copy and paste. Write in clear Arabic that Gulf viewers understand.

## Output, in this order
1. **Caption** (TikTok/Reels/Shorts): 3-5 short lines.
   - Line 1 repeats or sharpens the hook as a question or bold claim.
   - Lines 2-3: the problem and what the product does, only claims from the official site.
   - Last line: one CTA, "الرابط بالبايو".
   - Always end with `#إعلان (رابط عمولة)`.
   - At most 2 emoji.
2. **Hashtags**: 5-7. Mix 2 broad (#ذكاء_اصطناعي), 3 niche (#سيرة_ذاتية), 1-2 country (#وظائف_السعودية). No banned or unrelated trending tags.
3. **Pinned comment**: one line that invites a reply and repeats where the link is.
4. **Posting time**: default 8-11 pm Saudi time (UTC+3) for Gulf audiences; say it is a starting default to test, not a rule.
5. **Per-platform notes**:
   - TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Snap Spotlight: same 9:16 video; keep text out of the bottom 20% and the right edge (UI covers it).
   - YouTube Shorts: links in Shorts are not clickable; put the link in the channel and a pinned comment.
   - Pinterest: a still frame or a 2:3 image with the article link and a keyword-rich title.
6. **Link check**: the bio link must go to the person's own site page for this product, which holds the affiliate link and the disclosure, not straight to a raw affiliate URL.

## Rules
- Never invent prices, discounts, deadlines or statistics. If a fact is missing, write [تأكد من السعر] and flag it.
- Never write fake testimonials or claim personal use the person has not done; use "جرّبت" only if they did, otherwise "لقيت".
- Keep the disclosure even when the platform has its own paid-partnership toggle; tell the person to switch that toggle on too.
