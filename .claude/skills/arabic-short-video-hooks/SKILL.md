# Arabic short-video hooks and scripts

Use this when writing or fixing a vertical short video (15-45 s) that promotes a product, mostly digital tools, to Gulf and Arab viewers. The goal is completion and saves, then clicks on the link in bio. Never promise virality or income.

## Language
- Write spoken lines in clear white-dialect Arabic that Gulf viewers understand (mix of Saudi-friendly and Levantine is fine; avoid heavy local slang and formal fusha).
- On-screen text: 3-7 words, one idea, no more than 2 lines.
- Numbers as digits (3, 20, 10).

## Step 1: generate 6-10 hooks, at least 3 archetypes
The hook is the first 1-3 seconds. Write it in three layers: what the first frame shows, the spoken line, the on-screen text.

Archetypes:
1. Pain question: names a pain the viewer has right now. "بعتت سيرتك لعشرين شركة وما حدا ردّ؟"
2. Contrarian: says the common belief is wrong. "المشكلة مش بخبرتك، المشكلة بطريقة الكتابة"
3. Hidden cost: what the viewer loses without knowing. "كل يوم بتضيع ساعة على شغلة بتخلص بدقيقة"
4. Specific result: a concrete outcome with a number. "سيرة ذاتية بالعربي والإنكليزي بـ 3 دقايق"
5. Middle of action: start mid-demo, no intro. First frame already shows the tool working.
6. Investigator: "جربت 5 أدوات، وحدة بس تستاهل"
7. Audience call-out: names who it is for. "إذا عم تدوّر شغل بالسعودية، شوف هاد"

Rules:
- Specific beats clever: concrete nouns, odd numbers, named stakes.
- The hook must match what the video delivers. No bait-and-switch.
- Banned openings: greetings, "أهلاً فيكم", logo animations, "لا تنسوا الاشتراك", vague teases ("رح تنصدم").
- Mark the strongest 2 hooks and say why in one line each.

## Step 2: script structure (hook, escalate, payoff, CTA)
| Part | Time | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0-3 s | Stop the scroll (Step 1) |
| Problem | 3-7 s | Make the pain concrete, one sentence |
| Demo / proof | 7-22 s | Show the product doing 2-3 things; one feature per scene; before/after when possible |
| Offer | 22-26 s | Price, free trial, or what is free; honest |
| CTA | last 3-4 s | One action only: "الرابط بالبايو". Optional second: save or send to a friend |

Retention rules:
- A new visual every 2-4 seconds (scene change, zoom, new text).
- Each scene has one subtitle line that matches the voice word for word.
- Open a small loop early and close it at the end ("والميزة التالتة أهم وحدة").
- Cut every word that does not move the viewer forward.

## Step 3: honesty and compliance
- Every promo video and caption carries #إعلان and says the link is an affiliate link.
- Only claim features the product's official site states. No invented statistics, no fake reviews, no fake urgency.
- No health, finance-returns, or guaranteed-results claims.
- Do not use brand logos or product images unless they come from the affiliate program's official creatives.

## Output format
1. Hooks list (6-10), grouped by archetype, best 2 marked.
2. Full script as a table: scene number, seconds, first-frame visual, spoken line, on-screen text.
3. One-line note on what to test next time (e.g. try a contrarian hook vs the pain question).
